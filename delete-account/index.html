Delete your ReceiptWhere account or receipts

ReceiptWhere is provided by DuoNex AI LLC, Burien, Washington, USA.

Delete in the app

  • One receipt: swipe it to the left in your list (you can undo for a few seconds), or open it, tap ⋯, then Delete.
  • Your whole account: open Account → Delete account, read what will be deleted, and confirm.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or receipts. Use Delete account first.
  • Deleting your account also removes ReceiptWhere's files from your phone.

No access to the app?

Email receipt@receiptwhere.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll reply to that address to confirm the request is yours, and delete your account promptly after you confirm, no later than 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All your receipts and documents, including the original files in both of our storage locations
  • The details we read from them, your confirmations, projects and links between documents
  • Your account, your sign-in sessions and your forwarding address (which stops accepting email)
  • The notification registrations of your phones

What we keep, and why

  • A one-way fingerprint of your account and forwarding address. It can't be turned back into your data. We keep it so your forwarding address is never given to anyone else, and so your deletion is re-applied if we ever restore a backup.
  • Cost records, with no link to you or your documents.
  • Copies in our backups expire within 35 days, after which no copy remains.
  • Copies held briefly by the services that read your receipts (Anthropic and Google) and by our email provider expire under their own retention periods, within days to weeks.
  • Our service logs contain no receipt contents; they expire within 90 days.

Timing

Deletion in the app takes effect immediately in our live systems. Copies in our backups expire within 35 days.
