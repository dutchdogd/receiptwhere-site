Questions

What happens at the 51st receipt on Free?

Nothing is lost. The receipt is kept and read at the start of next month, or right away when you upgrade.

Does ReceiptWhere do my taxes?

No. It gives you, or your tax preparer, checked records and one clean export. It doesn't give tax advice.

How do I cancel?

In Google Play's subscriptions. You keep the paid plan until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your account also stops the renewal.

Do you sell my data or show ads?

No. Your receipts are only used to run ReceiptWhere for you. See the Privacy Policy.

Is there an iPhone app?

It's coming soon after Android. Your account and receipts will work on both.
