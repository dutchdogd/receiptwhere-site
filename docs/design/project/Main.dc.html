How it works

Three steps. You only do the first.

  1. Step 1

    Capture

    Scan a paper receipt, import photos and PDFs, share from any app, or forward email receipts to your own ReceiptWhere address.

  2. Step 2

    ReceiptWhere reads and checks

    Store, date, total, tax and payment method are read automatically, then checked against the receipt's own numbers. Anything uncertain lands in "To check".

  3. Step 3

    Find, total, export

    Search any receipt, see totals by month, store, card or project, and export a clean file for your tax preparer or bookkeeper.

What makes it different

It tells you when it isn't sure.

Most apps read a receipt and hope for the best. ReceiptWhere checks every total against what the receipt itself proves: the items, the tax, and the payment. When the numbers don't agree, or a faded digit could be read two ways, it asks you instead of guessing. One tap fixes it, and your correction is kept.

  • ✓Total checked against subtotal plus tax, and against the payment

  • ✓The same receipt sent twice is spotted, so nothing counts double
  • ✓A notification when a receipt needs a quick check
  • ✓Your original image is always kept, next to what was read
