Features

From paper to proof, without typing a single number.

Capture

Every way a receipt reaches you

  • Scan paper

    The camera finds the edges and straightens the receipt for you.

  • Import photos and PDFs

    Bring in the receipts already on your phone, several at once.

  • Share from any app

    Send a receipt to ReceiptWhere from your gallery, files or email app.

  • Your own forwarding address

    Forward email receipts, or set a filter, and they arrive by themselves.

Read and check

Read like a bookkeeper would

  • The details that matter

    Store, date, total, tax, and how you paid: card, debit, EBT, gift card or cash.

  • Checked against the receipt

    The total has to agree with the items, the tax and the payment before it counts.

  • Refunds and returns

    Recognised as money back, also when the store prints the minus sign at the end.

  • No double counting

    The same receipt scanned twice, or the email and the paper copy, is spotted.

  • "To check" and notifications

    When something is uncertain, you get a quiet notification and a one-tap check.

  • "Something's wrong"

    Correct any detail on any receipt. Your correction is kept, even when it's read again.

Organise and use

Find it, total it, hand it over

  • Search

    By store, amount, date or anything printed on the receipt.

  • Projects

    Group receipts per job, client, trip or renovation, with a running total.

  • Totals

    Per month, store, card or project, with refunds netted.

  • Export for your tax preparer

    One CSV for the year, with each receipt marked as checked or confirmed. On the paid plan.
