## 1. The service

ReceiptWhere lets you collect, store, organise and find your own financial documents, such as receipts, invoices, card slips, refunds and credit notes. You can photograph, upload or share documents in the app, or forward them by email to your personal ReceiptWhere address. We read each document automatically to identify details such as the merchant, total, tax, currency, date and payment method.

ReceiptWhere is currently in **beta**. Features may change, and the service may occasionally be unavailable.

# ReceiptWhere Privacy Policy

*Effective November 10, 2026. This policy explains how DuoNex AI LLC ("DuoNex", "we") handles your information in ReceiptWhere. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.*

## 1. What we collect

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account | Email address; the account identifier Google or Apple gives us if you sign in with them; time zone; home country; plan; your personal inbound address |
| Your documents | Photos and PDFs you upload or share, and emails you forward: the email's sender, subject and date, its attachments that are documents, and its text when the email itself is the receipt |
| Details read from documents | Merchant, total, tax, currency, date, purchase or refund, payment method and the last four digits of a card or account when printed. When an email names no merchant, we may suggest one from the sender's address for you to confirm. |
| Your confirmations | Corrections and confirmations you make in the app, and receipts you mark as wrong, with the date and time |
| Technical | Sign-in records (sign-in links and sessions, stored in hashed form), delivery records for forwarded email (in hashed form), error and request logs (which include IP addresses), and per-document processing cost records |
| Notifications | If you turn on notifications: a push token for this app on your device, the platform (Android or iOS), the app version, and when the device last registered |

Attachments we do not read, such as logos, signatures, unsupported files and files over 20 MB, are not kept: we note only their name and size. Mail sent to an address that belongs to no account is not kept. Mail our email provider flags as spam is not read. We keep only its sender, subject and date, for 7 days.

We do not ask for, and you should not upload, full card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers. Card numbers printed on receipts are almost always masked by the merchant. When a document shows a full account or membership number, the text we store and search keeps only its last four digits. Your original image or file is kept unchanged, as your record.

Notifications are off until you turn them on. A notification only says that a receipt needs a check: it never contains an amount, a store, a date or card details.

## 2. How we use it

- To provide ReceiptWhere: storing your documents, reading them automatically, showing and searching them, and letting you correct details.
- To sign you in and send you service emails, such as sign-in links. We do not send marketing email without your consent.
- To tell you, if you turn on notifications, when a receipt needs a quick check.
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, enforce plan limits and control costs.
- To improve the reading of documents, using our own test documents and measurements. We do not use your documents to build marketing profiles.

## 4. How long we keep it

- While your account is open, we keep your documents and details. On the Free plan, documents older than six months are archived, not deleted, and become visible again if you upgrade.
- Delivery records for forwarded email are deleted after 48 hours, and used or expired sign-in links after 24 hours.
- Notifications: the push token is deleted when you turn notifications off, sign out or delete your account, and otherwise after 270 days without use. Records of sent notifications are kept for 30 days.
- **When you delete your account,** we remove your documents, forwarded emails, details, confirmations, sign-in records, notification registrations and inbound address from our live systems immediately. Copies in our backups expire within 35 days. Technical logs contain no document contents and are kept for up to 90 days. If another account uploaded an identical file, that copy stays with that account only. Cost records are kept without any link to you or your documents. We keep a one-way fingerprint of a deleted account and its inbound address, so that the address is never given to anyone else and the deletion is re-applied if a backup is ever restored. Copies held briefly by our reading providers and our email provider expire under their own retention periods, within days to weeks. See receiptwhere.com/delete-account.
- Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

## 5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Files are stored in private storage that is not publicly accessible, and access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.
