ReceiptWhere Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026. This policy explains how DuoNex AI LLC ("DuoNex", "we") handles your information in ReceiptWhere. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

1. What we collect

CategoryExamples
AccountEmail address; the account identifier Google or Apple gives us if you sign in with them; time zone; home country; plan; your personal inbound address
Your documentsPhotos and PDFs you upload, and emails you forward: the email's sender, subject and date, its attachments that are documents, and its text when the email itself is the receipt
Details read from documentsMerchant, total, currency, date, purchase or refund, payment method and the last four digits of a card when printed. When an email names no merchant, we may suggest one from the sender's address for you to confirm.
Your confirmationsCorrections and confirmations you make in the app, with the date and time
TechnicalSign-in records (sign-in links and sessions, stored in hashed form), delivery records for forwarded email (in hashed form), error and request logs (which include IP addresses), and per-document processing cost records

Attachments we do not read, such as logos, signatures, unsupported files and files over 20 MB, are not kept: we note only their name and size. Mail sent to an address that belongs to no account is not kept. Mail our email provider flags as spam is not read. We keep only its sender, subject and date, for 7 days.

We do not ask for, and you should not upload, full card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers. Card numbers printed on receipts are almost always masked by the merchant.

2. How we use it

  • To provide ReceiptWhere: storing your documents, reading them automatically, showing and searching them, and letting you correct details.
  • To sign you in and send you service emails, such as sign-in links. We do not send marketing email without your consent.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, enforce plan limits and control costs.
  • To improve the reading of documents, using our own test documents and measurements. We do not use your documents to build marketing profiles.

4. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your documents and details. On the Free plan, documents older than six months are archived, not deleted, and become visible again if you upgrade.
  • Delivery records for forwarded email are deleted after 48 hours, and used or expired sign-in links after 24 hours.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your documents, forwarded emails, details, confirmations, sign-in records and inbound address from our live systems immediately. Copies in our backups expire within 35 days. Technical logs are kept up to 90 days. If another account uploaded an identical file, that copy stays with that account only. Cost records are kept without any link to you or your documents. We keep a one-way fingerprint of a deleted account and its inbound address, so that the address is never given to anyone else and the deletion is re-applied if a backup is ever restored.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Files are stored in private storage that is not publicly accessible, and access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: view and correct your documents' details in the app.
  • Export: on request, we provide a copy of your data on any plan. Downloads in the app (PDF, CSV and UTEM files) will come with paid plans.
  • Deletion: delete your whole account in the app. To delete a single document, contact us and we will delete it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as objecting to processing or asking for a copy of your data in a portable format. Contact us to use them.

7. International users

ReceiptWhere is operated from the United States, and your data is stored in North America. If you use it from another country, your data is transferred there. Before we offer ReceiptWhere in the EU, we will put the required transfer safeguards in place and update this policy.

8. Children

ReceiptWhere is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
